# Integrations

Because members log in to the portal, it can send them on to other services
already identified, and it is the place those services ask about members.

- [Programme](programme.md): joining programme items on Zoom Events, streams
  or Discord, and sign-up forms
- [Discord](discord.md): joining the convention's server, roles in the server
  and programme item chat
- [Watson](watson.md): the Discord bot that gives members roles and creates
  programme item posts
- [ConClár](conclar.md): keeping members' starred items on the portal
- [Business meeting](business-meeting.md): members' personal join links for
  an online WSFS Business Meeting, and the MPC election
- [The API](api.md): how other systems call the portal

Logging in through an identity provider or looking members up in a
registration system is covered under [Logging in](../logging-in.md), and
linking to other sites with the member's details under
[Linking to other sites](../cards.md#linking-to-other-sites).
